Model comparison at a glance

Model Predicts Advantage Limitation
Logistic Regression Purchase/churn probability Explainable binary baseline Misses nonlinear relationships
Decision Tree / Random Forest Purchase/offer response Captures nonlinear patterns Overfitting or opacity
K-Means Customer segments Finds behavioral groups Requires choice of k
Collaborative Filtering Product/content relevance Uses user-item interactions Cold-start problem
Neural Networks Complex personalized ranking Flexible representation learning High data and compute needs
ARIMA / LSTM Future aggregate demand Temporal forecasting Sudden shifts are difficult
