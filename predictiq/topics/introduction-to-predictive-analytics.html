01 / 01

Definition

Predictive analytics uses historical and current data with statistical or machine-learning models to estimate future outcomes. It gives a probability, not certainty.

01 / 02

Why preferences matter

Customers differ in product taste, price sensitivity, timing and channel. Better forecasts can reduce irrelevant messages and improve customer experience.

01 / 03

Traditional vs predictive marketing

Traditional: broad demographic campaigns and last-month reporting. Predictive: score an individual’s next likely purchase, test targeted experiences and learn from outcomes.
