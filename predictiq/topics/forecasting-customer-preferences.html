05 / 01

Purchase prediction

Estimate P(buy within 7 days) using prior purchases, page views and cart behavior. Choose a threshold based on costs and benefits.

Applied example: A customer viewed running shoes 12 times but has not purchased. A calibrated model estimates likelihood; a separate decision policy selects whether to recommend, remind or do nothing.
